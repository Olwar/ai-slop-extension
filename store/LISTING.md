# Chrome Web Store listing

**Name:** AI Writing Signs

**Short description (≤132 chars):**
Highlights telltale signs of AI-generated writing on any page. Hover to see why. One click to turn off.

**Detailed description:**
AI Writing Signs marks the phrases that commonly give away machine-written prose, directly on the page you are reading.

What it flags (based on Wikipedia's "Signs of AI writing"):
• Negative parallelisms: "It's not X, it's Y", "not only… but also"
• "No X, no Y, no Z" chains, with a count badge
• Dramatic one-liners: "Sit with that.", "That's not nothing."
• Puffery: "stands as a testament", "nestled in the heart of", "vibrant community"
• AI vocabulary: delve, tapestry, pivotal, underscore, showcase…
• Vague attribution: "Experts argue", "widely regarded"
• Filler and hedging: "It's important to note", "In conclusion"
• Chatbot leftovers: "As an AI language model", "I hope this helps!"

Strong signals get a deep yellow; weaker ones stay light. Hover any mark to see the reason.

Easy to turn off: a global switch, a per-site switch, a toggle for weak signals, and the Alt+Shift+A shortcut. The toolbar badge shows the number of marks on the page.

Everything runs locally. No data leaves your browser. No accounts, no tracking.

**Category:** Productivity
**Single purpose:** Highlights common signs of AI-generated writing on web pages.

**Permission justifications:**
- storage: saves the on/off state, the weak-signal setting, and the list of disabled sites.
- activeTab: reads the current tab's hostname for the per-site switch.
- Host permissions (http://*/*, https://*/*): the content script must run on every page to mark text in place.

**Data use:** Does not collect or transmit any user data.
